Frequently Asked Questions

What are our credentials?

All of our Game Masters have valid current Working with Children Checks, First aid + CPR and experience working with children.

My child has never played D&D. Will they be lost?

Nope! Our sessions are designed agnostic of the students experience and tailored to their learning goals

Where and when do sessions run?

We bring the learning to you, sessions run in your home no equipment required.

Isn't this just a games club with a label on it?

Every campaign starts with a written list of reading, writing and social objectives for that group — open any quest in Adventures In Progress and check the Learning tab. Objectives are ticked off only when a player actually demonstrates them at the table, and you get a record of your own child's progress, not just the party's.

What ages do you teach?

Ages 8 to 18.

Do I have to commit to a whole term?

Nope! You can book a single session first and expand to a full term at anytime.

Is D&D too dark or scary for young kids?

Nope! Our stories are designed specifically for the age groups that engage with them. Our Game Masters are always ready to talk to parents and students to keep the story and learnings within expectations.

Can you run sessions for our school or service?

Yes. We offer on-site sessions for schools, libraries, OOSH programs and support services, mapped to your literacy goals with written reporting on request. Use the form below and mention your organisation.

Ready to begin your adventure?

Tell us about your young adventurer — their age, their interests, and what you'd like them to get better at. We'll tell you honestly whether we've got the right table for them.
